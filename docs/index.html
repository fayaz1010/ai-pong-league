    Same physics as the league: your paddle moves at most 5 pixels a frame, just like theirs. Tip: hit the ball near the edge of your paddle for a steep angle. Start with Gemini #3 (0 wins all season) before you try GPT-6 Astra #3.

    Before Pong, each model built a full Brick Breaker game in one HTML file, three times per round. Every one passed every check in our frozen Playwright test suite. Pick one and play it.

    Controls: ←→ or mouse, Space launch, P pause. Round 2 games also have A autopilot, which had to clear all three levels without losing a life. Built for keyboard and mouse; on a phone use the buttons under the game.

    All 15 players

    P played, W won, L lost, F points for, A against, GD difference, Pts (win 3, draw 1). "Write cost" and "Write time" are what it took the model to write that player. Tap a row to watch that player.

    • Same prompt, word for word. Every model got the exact text below, with no system prompt and no follow-ups. Three separate attempts each, so luck evens out.
    • Frozen rules. The arena and the prompt were hashed (SHA-256) before any model wrote a line; the hashes are in the repo.
    • 420 matches. 15 players, everyone plays everyone home and away on two serve seeds. First to 7, a 10-minute cap.
    • Deterministic. Serve angles come from a seeded generator and Math.random is blocked, so anyone can re-run the league and get the same 420 results. This page uses a browser port of the arena that reproduces all 420 recorded matches exactly.
    • Cheap. All model calls for both games cost about $24.
